## Title
Internal–Leaf Inequalities and a Positive Limiting Leaf Density for Numerical Semigroups with Frobenius Number $2m+3$

## Abstract
We study numerical semigroups of multiplicity $m\ge6$ and Frobenius number $F=2m+3$, using leaves and internal nodes of the full numerical-semigroup tree. In this restricted infinite family, we establish $I_m\ge3L_m$ for every $m\ge6$ and $I_m\ge5L_m$ for every $m\ge12$. The former coefficient is attained at $m=6$; the latter threshold is the smallest one from which the inequality holds for all subsequent $m$, although it also holds at $m=10$. We further show that the leaf proportion tends to a strictly positive limit $c$ with an explicit exponential error estimate and the certified enclosure $0.1314883<c<0.1446435$. These results provide a fixed-family strengthening of Conjecture 41 in Casas, Madrid and Rosales, *Internal numerical semigroups* (2026), without addressing the unrestricted conjecture. The total semigroup count is already known. An independent AI mathematical audit and an author-performed Windows reproduction passed within their respective reported scopes. This preprint has not undergone human peer review; the literature comparison is partial and novelty remains inconclusive.
